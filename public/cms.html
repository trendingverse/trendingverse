The problem

Indian publishers are running
five tools where one should do.

WordPress for publishing, Google Ad Manager for ads, Analytics for audience, a separate SEO tool, and still no direct way to sell ads to local advertisers.

💸

AdSense pays pennies for Indian traffic

CPMs for Indian-language audiences on AdSense average ₹20–60. Direct ad deals with local brands pay 5–10× more — but there's no platform to run them.

✍️

AI tools don't write in your language

ChatGPT writes passable Kannada or Tamil, but it doesn't understand Google Discover, SEO scoring, or your editorial workflow. It's a text box, not a publishing tool.

📉

No visibility into your audience

WordPress tells you page views. It doesn't tell you which readers scroll to the bottom, which cities your audience comes from, or how many have shared their email.

🔄

Publishing is still manual

Write in Google Docs, paste into WordPress, add SEO fields, find a photo, set the category, check the preview. Every single article. Every single day.
